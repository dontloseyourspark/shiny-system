AgentAmina

Transforming Police Services for Enhanced Trust and Engagement

Overview

AgentAmina was a recruitment case study for Deloitte. The brief was to design a digital companion that helps marginalized individuals navigate their interactions with law enforcement, understand their rights, and report incidents safely.

I researched the problem, defined the opportunity and pain points, created a persona, and designed and prototyped the solution, then presented it as part of the interview process.

Category
Public Safety
Platform
Mobile app
Context
Recruitment case for Deloitte

The goal

Design a user-centered and accessible experience that enhances trust and engagement across Dutch Police platforms and services.

The solution

AgentAmina is a digital companion designed to help marginalized individuals navigate their interactions with law enforcement, understand their rights, and report incidents safely.
