Creative Pitch

The Fill Up Squad

Interactive Fuel Rewards

Overview

As part of a creative pitch to a leading petrol company in South Africa, I proposed an interactive rewards concept that brings fuel stops to life. The idea centred around turning each petrol station into a discovery point where users hunt for unique QR codes — each one unlocking a different animal character and a surprise reward.

This game-like experience encourages exploration across multiple locations, and users are further incentivised by logging trips, arriving at destinations, and trading accumulated points for physical rewards like plush toys or fuel discounts. Designed to delight all ages, the concept connects physical spaces to digital engagement in a way that's playful, rewarding, and brand-building.
