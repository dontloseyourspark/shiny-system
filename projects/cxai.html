CXAI

AI-driven SaaS platform for customer support teams

Overview

CXAI was a recruitment case study for 5CA. The brief was to design a modular, role-based dashboard and landing page for CXAI, an AI-driven SaaS platform for customer support teams.

I reviewed comparable platforms, defined three personas, and wireframed and designed a dashboard aimed at streamlining agent workflows and reducing response time.

Category
AI SaaS
Platform
Web
Context
Recruitment case for 5CA

The goal

Design a modular, role-based dashboard and landing page for CXAI — an AI-driven SaaS platform for customer support teams.

The solution

My design introduced a clean layout, contextual navigation, and smart shortcuts to reduce cognitive load and improve efficiency. By prioritising frequently used actions and making system feedback more intuitive, the interface is designed to be fast and easy for agents to use in high-pressure support environments.
