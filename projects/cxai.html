Case Study · AI SaaS

CXAI

AI-driven SaaS platform for customer support teams

Overview

I designed an internal support tool for 5CA to streamline agent workflows and reduce response time. Through user research and iterative prototyping, I delivered a solution that improved usability, minimised training time, and aligned with the company’s global remote-first model.

Category
AI SaaS
Platform
Web
For
5CA

The goal

Design a modular, role-based dashboard and landing page for CXAI — an AI-driven SaaS platform for customer support teams.

The solution

My design introduced a clean layout, contextual navigation, and smart shortcuts to reduce cognitive load and improve efficiency. By prioritising frequently used actions and making system feedback more intuitive, the interface is fast and easy for agents to use in high-pressure support environments.
