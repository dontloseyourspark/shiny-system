Soldibank

Whitelabel Mobile Banking App

Overview

Soldibank is a whitelabel mobile banking app I designed at BBD. We wanted a whitelabel banking solution that could showcase our backend and how it integrated with software made by our partners, in this case Thought Machine.

We could already theme our Vanguard software fairly easily to get the correct look, so what we needed was a banking application that would showcase the user interacting with our products. That's how this application was designed.
